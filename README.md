# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the sales and profitability of an online garden retail business using SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

The objective is to explore business data, identify sales trends, evaluate product and category performance, and generate actionable business insights to support better decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies

- **SQL (DuckDB):** Data exploration, querying, joins, aggregations, and analysis.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis, pivot tables, KPI summaries, and visualisations.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualisation.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains information about an online garden retailer and consists of seven tables:

| Table | Description |
|---|---|
| customers | Customer information |
| products | Product details and pricing |
| categories | Product categories |
| orders | Order information and status |
| order_items | Products and quantities in each order |
| payments | Customer payment information |
| returns | Product return information |

### Dataset Size

| Table | Records |
|---|---:|
| Categories | 24 |
| Customers | 12,000 |
| Products | 2,000 |
| Orders | 90,000 |
| Order Items | 215,000 |
| Payments | 95,000 |
| Returns | 12,000 |

## Key Business Insights

- **Category Performance:** Furniture recorded the highest profit margin at 42.67%.
- **Sales Channel:** The Web channel generated the highest revenue of 5,225,016.97.
- **Top-Selling Product:** Heritage Steel Trowel recorded 68,492 units sold and revenue of 915,088.03.
- **Product Returns:** Heritage Steel Trowel also recorded the highest number of returns, with 4,541.
- **Order Status:** Delivered orders accounted for 64,897 of the 90,000 total orders.
- **Payment Analysis:** Card was the most frequently used payment method, with 64,212 payments.
- **Country Analysis:** The United States recorded 15,470 orders and revenue of 1,566,399.36.

## Business Recommendations

1. Improve the online shopping experience to strengthen Web channel sales.
2. Focus on profitable categories such as Furniture.
3. Investigate product return reasons, especially for high-return products.
4. Identify the causes of order cancellations and improve fulfilment.
5. Maintain secure and convenient payment options.
6. Strengthen business presence in important geographical markets.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL, Excel, and Power BI can be used together to transform raw business data into meaningful insights.

The analysis helps identify revenue drivers, profitable categories, customer payment preferences, product performance, and potential areas for improvement.

The interactive dashboard and business insights provide a foundation for data-driven decision-making.
